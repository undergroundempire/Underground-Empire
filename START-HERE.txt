UNDERGROUND EMPIRE — WEBSITE PACKAGE

FAST WEBSITE + CINEMATIC GIF INTRO

UPLOAD CONTENTS
1. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to the ROOT of your GitHub Pages repository (same level as index.html).
2. Keep the assets folder.
3. Add your own Bohee photo as: bohee.jpg (same folder as index.html).
4. Add your crown image as: Crown.png (same folder as index.html).
5. Add your original audio as: Underground Empire with intro.mp3 (same folder as index.html).

IMPORTANT
- The animated intro artwork is optimized as WebP to reduce loading time.
- Visitors can tap ENTER THE EMPIRE to start the MP3 (mobile browsers require a tap for sound). They can also Skip intro.
- The site automatically opens after 4.5 seconds if the visitor does not tap.
- Your Bohee photo, Crown.png and MP3 were not present in the ZIP files available for this build, so add your original files using the exact names above.
- Admin upload requires your own Firebase project settings. Open firebase-config.js and follow SETUP-ANDROID.md. Do not publish Firebase admin credentials.
- For the fastest first load, keep media files reasonably compressed and avoid uploading huge uncompressed videos.
